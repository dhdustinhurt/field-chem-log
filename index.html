Chemicals

Starter data for Nebraska corn, soybean and cover-crop programs. Product names, active ingredients and typical rates are here to save you typing — EPA numbers and a.i. concentrations still have to be checked against the jug you actually have. They differ between generics, formulations and label revisions. Anything added from here is flagged verify label until you confirm it.

Export & backup

Excel workbook

Six sheets: every product line, active ingredient detail, one row per pass, season a.i. totals by field, plus your fields and chemicals.
On iPhone/iPad the share sheet opens — choose Save to Files, or send it straight to Mail.

Season limits lb a.i. per acre

Set a per-season cap on any active ingredient and the app warns you when a new pass would push a field over it. Caps are per a.i., so every product containing it counts — atrazine in a premix adds to straight atrazine.

Backup

Everything lives only on this device. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. If the phone is lost or the app is deleted, the data goes with it — save a backup file somewhere now and then.
